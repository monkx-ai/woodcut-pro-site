Data stored on your device

WoodCut Pro stores the following information locally in the app's own private storage on your device, using a local database and local preferences file. None of it is transmitted to us, because the app has no backend server to send it to.

Project & cut-list data

  • Project names and notes you enter
  • Part dimensions, quantities, and labels you add to a sheet or lumber cut list
  • Materials you save to your Materials Library (names, dimensions, prices, grain settings)
  • Computed cutting layouts and cost estimates, generated on-device from the above

App preferences

  • Your chosen measurement unit (mm, cm, inches, fractional inches) and fraction precision
  • Your default saw kerf setting
  • Your light/dark theme preference
  • Whether you've unlocked the Pro upgrade (see "Purchases & Google Play Billing" below)

You can view, edit, and delete any of this data at any time from within the app, and all of it is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data we don't collect

WoodCut Pro does not include any analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and we — the developer — never see, collect, or sell your personal data. The one exception is the advertising SDK described in the next section, used only in the free version of the app. Specifically, the app does not collect or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account/profile information — there is no sign-in
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Crash logs or diagnostic data sent to us (your device's own OS-level crash reporting, if enabled, is controlled by Android/Google, not by this app)
  • Usage analytics of any kind

Advertising & your ad choices

The free version of WoodCut Pro shows banner ads on several screens, and an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad after finishing a sheet or lumber cutting plan, both served by Google AdMob. Interstitial ads are shown infrequently (roughly once every few completed plans), and never while you're actively editing a cut list or viewing a cutting diagram. If you purchase the one-time Pro upgrade, all ads — banner and interstitial — are removed from every screen.

What AdMob collects

To select and serve ads, Google's AdMob SDK can collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, general region derived from IP address), and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks). This is handled directly by Google's SDK running inside the app — WoodCut Pro itself does not separately collect or store this information, and none of it reaches our own servers, because we don't operate any.

Consent (EEA, UK, and similar regions)

For users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and other regions with similar consent requirements, WoodCut Pro uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form the first time you open the app. Ads are only personalized, and the advertising ID is only used for ad personalization, according to the choices you make in that form. You can change your consent choices at any time from Settings. Outside of regions where consent is legally required, ads may be shown without this prompt, consistent with Google's own policies.

Your choices

  • Reset or change your ad consent choice at any time via the consent form, or by resetting advertising ID consent in your device's Google Settings app.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising entirely for your Google account at adssettings.google.com.
  • Reset or disable your device's advertising ID from Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Purchase the Pro upgrade to remove ads from the app entirely.

AdMob's data collection and use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and, for advertising specifically, Google's AdMob data disclosure.

Purchases & Google Play Billing

WoodCut Pro offers an optional, one-time "Pro" upgrade purchased through Google Play Billing. When you make this purchase:

  • The entire payment flow — including your card or payment method details — is handled by Google Play. The app never sees, requests, or stores your payment information.
  • Google shares with the app only the purchase result (success/failure) and an entitlement token used to confirm you own the Pro upgrade.
  • We store a simple local flag on your device recording that Pro is unlocked, so the app can enable Pro features without contacting a server of ours.

Google's handling of your payment data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

Exporting & sharing PDFs

WoodCut Pro can generate a PDF cutting plan from your project data. This PDF is created entirely on your device. If you choose to share it — for example via email, messaging, or a cloud storage app — that happens through Android's standard share sheet, and the destination is whatever app or contact you select. WoodCut Pro does not upload, email, or transmit the PDF on its own; sharing only happens if and when you tap "Share."

Backups & data deletion

WoodCut Pro supports Android's standard device backup system. If you have Android's built-in backup enabled on your device (a setting controlled by you and Google, not by this app), your project data and preferences may be included in that backup so they can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices. This backup is managed entirely by the Android operating system under Google's own privacy practices — WoodCut Pro does not operate or have access to any backup storage itself.

To delete your data:

  • Delete an individual project or material from within the app at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove all locally stored data from your device.
  • Clear the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → WoodCut Pro → Storage to wipe local data without uninstalling.

Third-party services

WoodCut Pro integrates two third-party services, both from Google:

  • Google Play Billing — used solely to process the optional Pro purchase, as described above.
  • Google AdMob (with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent) — used only in the free version of the app to show banner and interstitial ads, as described in "Advertising" above.

WoodCut Pro does not integrate any analytics provider, crash-reporting SDK, or social media SDK.

Children's privacy

WoodCut Pro is a professional/hobbyist woodworking tool, not directed at children, and its Play Store listing is not targeted at a child audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We have not configured any age-specific ad settings, so if you are a parent concerned about a child using this app, we recommend not installing the free, ad-supported version for them.
