Accuracy of calculations — important

Always verify measurements before cutting

WoodCut Pro's cutting diagrams, part layouts, board-foot and cost estimates, and other calculations are provided as a planning aid only. They depend entirely on the dimensions, kerf, and material settings you enter. Woodworking involves real material cost, sharp tools, and physical risk, so before you cut:

  • Double-check all dimensions and quantities against your own measurements and plans.
  • Verify your saw's actual kerf width and account for your own cutting technique.
  • Follow the safety instructions and guards for your tools and equipment.
  • Use your own professional judgment — the App does not know your specific tools, materials, or project requirements.

We are not responsible for wasted material, incorrect cuts, project delays, property damage, or injury resulting from reliance on the App's calculations. See Limitation of Liability below.
