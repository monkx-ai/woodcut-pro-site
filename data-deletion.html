Delete Your Data

WoodCut Pro

Effective date: 10 November 2026  ·  Developer: Existenx

WoodCut Pro stores all of your data — projects, cut lists, materials, and settings — locally on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that holds a copy of anything you enter. That means you can delete your data yourself, immediately, without waiting on a request to us. This page explains exactly how.

How to delete your data

Delete a single project or material

  1. Open WoodCut Pro.
  2. Go to Home (for a project) or Materials (for a saved material).
  3. Select the item you want to remove and choose Delete.

This permanently removes that item from the app's local storage right away.

Delete all app data, keeping the app installed

  1. On your Android device, open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → WoodCut Pro → Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

This immediately and permanently erases every project, material, and setting stored by the app on your device.

Delete everything by uninstalling

  1. Press and hold the WoodCut Pro icon, or go to Settings → Apps → WoodCut Pro.
  2. Choose Uninstall.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its locally stored data as part of the normal Android uninstall process.

What is deleted, what isn't, and retention

Data typeDeleted by the steps above?Retention otherwise
Projects, cut lists, saved materials, app settings Yes — immediately, in full Kept only on your device for as long as the app is installed; never sent to us because we have no server
Pro purchase entitlement (Google Play Billing) No — this isn't app data, it's your purchase record with Google Play Retained by Google per Google Play's own policies; see Google Play support to manage or remove purchase history
Advertising ID / ad interaction data (free version only, via Google AdMob) No — this isn't held by WoodCut Pro at all; it's processed directly by Google Governed by Google's own retention policies; reset or opt out any time via adssettings.google.com or your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads — see our Privacy Policy for details

No account means no account to delete

WoodCut Pro does not support creating an account or signing in, so there is no username, password, email, or profile tied to you anywhere in our systems — we don't have a backend that could hold one. The deletion steps above cover everything the app itself stores.
